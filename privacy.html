Privacy Policy – Hermes Drive Sync

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What the app does

Hermes Drive Sync signs in to a Google account with the account owner's consent and synchronises one folder (hermes) of that account's Google Drive with a folder on a computer operated by the same owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission the app can read, create, modify and delete files in the owner's Google Drive. In practice it only handles the contents of the hermes folder: files the owner uploads for processing, the results produced from them and backup archives.

How data is used and stored

  • Files and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Data is used only to perform the synchronisation the owner has set up. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.
  • We do not run a server that receives, collects or stores Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in the owner's Drive and on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the local token and files from the computer.
