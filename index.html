Hermes Drive Sync

Hermes Drive Sync is a small private tool that keeps a single folder named hermes in the owner's own Google Drive in sync with a computer the owner controls. It is used by one person or one small office for its own files (documents to process, processed results and backups).

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only accesses the Google Drive of the Google account that signs in to it, and only to read and write files in that account's hermes folder.
